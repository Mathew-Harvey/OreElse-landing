A story in four acts.

You play Rigger Nineteen, a new hire on the moon Tithe. The company says the locals are friendly. The company has never been here. Between jobs, the game draws its cut scenes as comic panels in its own renderer.

Act 1 · Jobs 1 to 14

Dig

The Ironhold Charter against the Veyl. The yard, Flux, a second base, high ground, mines, fliers and the command view.

Act 2 · Jobs 15 to 26

Sing

Change sides and play the Veyl. Song fields, unseen bodies, the Chord and the Ferry.

Act 3 · Jobs 27 to 36

Grow

Play the Gloam. Buds and the Bloom, digging in, ripening, tunnels.

Act 4 · Jobs 37 to 42

Or Else

Each side against Charter Security, the company's own troops in grey. Then a last job against the clock.

Three sides, and none of them is reasonable.

Play any of them in the campaign, in a skirmish or online. Each has its own bodies, buildings, voice and bot brain.

Everything breaks.

Rocks crumble in stages and fuel drums set each other off. Rubble falls, bounces, piles up against walls and gets kicked when you walk through it. Tall towers fall on whatever is standing under them.

A browser demo is coming soon.

The full game is coming to Steam. The demo will run in a browser tab, with nothing to install. The models are sculpted in code and inked when the game boots, and a synth plays the music live.
